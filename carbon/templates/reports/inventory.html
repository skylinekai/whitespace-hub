Methodology and boundary

This inventory follows the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard and Scope 3 Standard, using an operational control boundary. Activity data (energy, fuel, distance, nights, waste tonnage, materials, and spend where no physical data exists) is multiplied by emission factors from {{ factor_set }}, the ICE database for construction materials and indicative spend-based factors. Well-to-tank and grid transmission losses for fuels and electricity are reported in Scope 3 Category 3.
